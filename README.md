# JobDrive

JobDrive is a private M2 internship discovery and tracking dashboard for France, focused on Data Science, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Signal Processing, Image Processing and adjacent DASSIP/CORO domains.

## Discovery coverage

JobDrive keeps two coverage concepts separate:

- **Discovery Sources** tracks the health of technical scanners such as employer ATS boards and France Travail.
- **Target Companies** tracks a strategic market universe of exactly 200 employers in France, classified as giants or recognized companies and prioritized by Tier 1–3.

Target-company coverage is evidence-based: `covered` requires a healthy mapped company-specific source scanned within 24 hours; `partial` requires an actual market-wide observation of that employer within 30 days; otherwise the company remains `uncovered`. Restricted sources such as LinkedIn or Indeed are never counted as covered.

The `Companies` dashboard view exposes target-company coverage, active retained internships and the uncovered-company backlog without weakening the existing M2 internship eligibility filters.

## Constraints

The project uses free infrastructure only. Academic/research-lab placements and defense-oriented opportunities are excluded from the target internship strategy, while opportunity-level France, internship, duration and technical-alignment gates remain mandatory.
